Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Malawi Directly is a business directory that helps people discover businesses and contact them. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the website.

1. Information we may collect

When a business submits a listing, we may collect its business name, category, district, location, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, description, opening hours, website or map details, and other information entered in the form. Advertising enquiries may include a contact name or business name, phone number, selected package, payment method and transaction reference.

2. How information is used

We use submitted information to review and publish business listings, operate the directory, respond to enquiries, manage advertising requests, verify payments manually, administer promotions and prevent misuse of the service.

3. Public information

Once a listing is approved, information included in that listing may be visible to anyone visiting the directory. Do not submit private information that you do not want displayed publicly. Do not submit another person's personal details unless you have permission.

4. Storage and service providers

Website listing information is stored using Google Firebase services. Information may be processed or stored on systems outside Malawi, depending on the service configuration. We do not ask users to enter mobile money PINs, passwords or one-time security codes into this website.

5. Sharing

We do not intend to sell submitted personal information. Information may be shared where needed to operate the website and its connected services, respond to a lawful request, protect users or investigate suspected fraud. Business contact details in approved listings are shared publicly as part of the directory.

6. Accuracy, access and removal requests

Business owners are responsible for ensuring their submitted information is accurate and that they are authorised to publish it. To request a correction or removal, contact the Malawi Directly administrator using the contact details shown on this website. We may ask for reasonable information to confirm that you are authorised to make the request.

7. Security and retention

We use access controls and administrative procedures intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to operate the directory, manage listings and advertising, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations.

8. Children and changes

This directory is intended for general business and public use, not for collecting children's personal information. We may update this policy when the service changes. The latest version will be displayed on this page.

Questions or privacy requests: use the Contact section of Malawi Directly.
